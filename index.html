Our services

Specialized care for pain and movement.

Explore testing and treatment options for nerve, muscle, and joint concerns.

Diagnostics

Electromyography

Testing to help explain numbness, tingling, or weakness in your arms or legs.

View service detailsHide service details

An EMG records electrical activity in a muscle while it rests and while you tighten it. It is often paired with a nerve conduction study.

Your clinician uses the results alongside your exam to help tell whether a nerve or muscle is involved and to guide the next step.

Treatment

Pain management

A plan for pain that interferes with work, sleep, or everyday movement.

View service detailsHide service details

We look at where you feel pain, how you move, and what you want to do more comfortably. Bring a list of treatments you’ve tried and how they worked for you.

Depending on your exam, we may discuss rehabilitation, changes to daily activities, or medication options. You can ask about benefits and trade-offs before deciding.

Treatment

Acupuncture

An option to consider for ongoing pain, alongside your rehabilitation plan.

View service detailsHide service details

Acupuncture uses very thin needles at selected points on the body. Some people find it helpful for certain types of pain, including persistent low back pain.

At your consultation, we’ll discuss your symptoms, what a session involves, and how you would judge whether treatment is helping.

Our approach

What would you like to do again?

It might be taking the stairs, getting through a workday, or playing a full game. That goal gives us a place to start.

Patient information

Before your first visit

What should I bring?

Bring a list of your medications and any relevant imaging reports, test results, or referral paperwork you already have. Write down one activity you’d like to make easier.

Do I need to choose a treatment first?

You can start with a general consultation. Tell us what has been difficult, and we’ll talk through whether testing, treatment, or another next step makes sense.

How do I check coverage or referral requirements?

Ask your insurer whether your plan requires a referral to a physical medicine and rehabilitation specialist. Confirm the office’s participation and any service-specific authorization before your appointment.
